With block 768978 I have finished my last drawing for the year 2022 - 'Bitcoin - Les Femmes Orange' - and it was an honor for me to artistically deal with the topic of women and Bitcoin.

I think it is needless to mention that the overwhelming majority of those individuals on our planet who are involved with Bitcoin are (still) male. Why is this so?

Well, I have two guesses: on the one hand, Bitcoin is crystal clear that it has something to do with finance, even if that is just the tip of the iceberg. I guess the finance/money investment section of the traditional fiat system is still a male domain and therefore fewer touch points for women with Bitcoin arise. Furthermore, Bitcoin seems to be very technical from a nocoiners point of view and the impression is created that you have to be extremely tech savvy to understand Bitcoin, which of course does not hurt, but is not absolutely necessary.

You very likely won't be able to explain the exact process and workings of an email, the internet, or the engine in your car, yet you use it. Since (IT) technology, computer science, and programming are also, by and large, more likely to pique the interest of men rather than women, the percentage of the male gender increases.

At the same time, Bitcoin is a perfect fit for women, not least because Bitcoin is completely neutral towards all users and network participants, but also out of emotional-human attitude and motherly instinct. While men can let their tinkerer's heart blossom with their fullnodes, mining devices, Tor, Bisq and LN Beer-Tabs, women - I think - would primarily recognize the fundamental value of Bitcoin and therefore draw interest.

Bitcoin is pure freedom and security. This is exactly what a person instinctively strives for. Especially a mother. Bitcoin provides immeasurable protection and (financial) security to a mother and her children for generations. Children are our legacy, gift to the future and what the future depends on. With Bitcoin, a mother has found a universal and very powerful tool to fulfill her maternal 'low-time-preference instinct'.

Of course, there are women who build an LN Beer Tab and men who save in Bitcoin solely for the long-term security of their son or daughter, but I think the motherhood aspect is not to be neglected and will lead many women, especially mothers, to Bitcoin. A woman doesn't have to become a mother to discover Bitcoin either! Bitcoin has barely been a concept for 14 years and there is still immeasurable room for self-realization through and with Bitcoin.

All things considered, women by nature have a very creative streak, are determined, willing to compromise, intelligent. These characteristics combined with the unimaginable amount of opportunities just scream for self-employment in the Bitcoin universe. I already know of several examples where a woman has realized herself with the help of Bitcoin and feels fulfillment in life. One such example is Rachel Geyer who, with the help of Debbi Reher and a handful of other ladies, is organizing a Bitcoin event by women for women.

Under the name "Les Femmes Orange", a long-term project to promote female involvement with Bitcoin is being launched at Germany's first and only Bitcoin hotel, Hotel Princess.

It is my firm belief that through more female interaction in the Bitcoin universe, some more, relevant and sustainable things about Bitcoin will be discovered and their real manifestation will happen for the good of all of us.

With these words, an appeal to all the ladies of the world: look at Bitcoin! In the end, a wonderful interaction is created, just like with anything and everything that deals with Bitcoin. Women empowering Bitcoin!
