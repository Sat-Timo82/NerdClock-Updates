In this work, which took about 125 hours, I was inspired by a magnificent statue of Archangel Michael, which can be seen triumphantly over Lucifer on the main entrance of St. Michael's Church in Hamburg.

In order not to create just a blunt copy, I have again incorporated some Bitcoin-related numbers, words and symbols that can be found throughout the drawing.

The main theme is, of course, Bitcoin's glorious victory over the monstrosity of our time, the fiat-inflation-credit/debt-money system that is robbing us of more and more quality of life.
